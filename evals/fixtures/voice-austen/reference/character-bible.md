# Characters

**Sophia Pellow** (22). The younger daughter of a clergyman's widow. Clever, quick to see what is ridiculous in others and slower to see it in herself. She reads too much and says a little more than she should.

**Louisa Pellow** (25). The elder sister: handsome, gentle, wholly without suspicion. She believes Mr. Haverstock admires her and is half in love with him.

**Mrs. Pellow**. A widow of narrow means (the family has about five hundred pounds and her small jointure) who has let the neighbourhood think them better off than they are, chiefly by never contradicting anyone.

**Mr. Gilbert Haverstock** (31). Lately come into a modest estate near Hartwell, encumbered with debts his father left. Civil, careful, and in want of money; not a villain.

**Mrs. Thornbury**. The widow who keeps Hartwell Lodge and the neighbourhood's news. She has told everyone the Pellows have six thousand pounds because she likes to have the largest news.

The setting is a small country town in the south of England, about 1810.
