# The Salt Ledger -- Systems Bible

---

## System 1: Bell-reading

### What It Is
The keepers of Kessin read the weather from the salt bells hung around the lamp gallery.

### Who Can Use It
Only someone who has kept a light for seven years can hear the flat note.

### How It Works
A salt bell rings flat when a storm is within a day of the island. Old Hallam puts it this way: "A flat bell never lies, but it never tells you twice."

### Rules
1. A bell rings flat only for a storm, never for fog.
2. Bells crack after about a hundred storms and must be recast on the mainland.

### Costs & Limits
Reading the bells costs sleep: a keeper who reads them through one night cannot read them the next.

### How It Feels
Like a tooth aching before the weather turns.

### Social Dimension
The Lighthouse Board does not recognise bell-reading; on the island everyone trusts it.

Bell-reading is the book's metaphor for listening to grief.
