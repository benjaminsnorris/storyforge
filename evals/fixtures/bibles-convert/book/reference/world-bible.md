# The Salt Ledger -- World Bible

## Overview

Kessin is a small salt island a day's sail from the mainland, in a cold northern sea. The island hoards silence the way it hoards salt.

---

## Geography & Setting

### The Macro
Winter storms come from November to March, out of the north-east. The ferry from the mainland calls twice a month, and not at all in January.

### Key Locations

**Kessin Light**
- A stone tower of forty-one steps on the island's northern point, above the shoals.

**The Salt Flats**
- The flats flood at every spring tide, and no one cuts salt while they are under water.
- The flats are the novel's image of erasure: whatever is written there, the tide takes.

**Harbor Office**
- The harbormaster's office on the quay, where the salt tax is paid.

---

## Politics & Power

### Laws & Governance
- The Lighthouse Board, on the mainland, appoints each keeper for a term of one year, renewed every spring.
- The salt tax is one crown a sack, paid at the Harbor Office.
- Only the harbormaster may record a ship as lost.
- A smuggler who is caught loses their boat.

### Political Tensions
Whether the Board knows about the smuggling is undecided.

---

## Daily Life

- Kessin has no doctor; the nearest is on the mainland.
- Everyone on the island buys from the one store, and the store keeps a tab.

---

## History

The island's people came for the salt two hundred years ago and never left. A history of small debts, never forgiven, runs under every family.
