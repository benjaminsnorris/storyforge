# The Salt Ledger -- Character Bible

---

## Primary Characters

### Mara Voss

**Role:** Protagonist. Keeper of Kessin Light.

**Age:** 34.

**Physical:**
- Tall and rawboned, with a burn scar across the back of her left hand from a lamp-oil fire.

**Psychological:**
- **Want:** To keep the light burning through the winter so the Lighthouse Board renews her post in the spring.
- **Need:** To stop paying her brother's debts as if they were her own.
- **Wound:** At nineteen she fell asleep on watch and the lamp went out; that night the *Grey Wren* broke on the shoals with six aboard.
- **Lie:** If she is never careless again, no one else will drown.
- **Flaw-as-Strength:** Her vigilance makes her the best keeper the island has had, and the loneliest.

**Voice Fingerprint:**
- Clipped, practical sentences. She counts things when she is anxious, and she never swears.
- "Forty-one steps. I've counted them every night for fifteen years."
- "The light doesn't care who's sorry."

**Arc:**
1. Mara treats the light as penance.
2. Brack's ledger shows her the wreck was not only her fault.
3. She learns that keeping watch is not the same as keeping faith.

Mara is the novel's emblem of duty curdled into penance; every scene at the lamp should feel like a confession.

---

### Teodor Brack

**Role:** Antagonist. Harbormaster of Kessin for twenty years, and the man who collects the salt tax.

**Physical:** Heavy, slow, always in the same oilskin coat.

**Psychological:**
- **Want:** To buy out the salt flats before the mainland company does.
- **Wound:** His father died in debt and the family boat was taken.

Brack holds the *Grey Wren*'s insurance: he was paid for the wreck, and he has never told anyone, Mara least of all.

**Voice Fingerprint:**
- He speaks in the terms of his ledger.
- "A debt is the only honest thing on this island."

Brack is less a villain than a mirror of Mara's own bookkeeping of guilt.

---

## Secondary Characters

### Ivo Voss

**Role:** Mara's younger brother, a salt cutter on the flats. He is 27.

**Profile:**
- Ivo owes Brack forty crowns from a winter of card games, and he has hidden the debt from Mara. Only Ivo and Brack know of it.
- He wants a boat of his own.
- Voice: he talks fast and jokes when he is cornered. "Everyone owes Brack something. I just owe him more interestingly."

He represents the island's restless young, for whom the light is a prison.

### Lise Anker

**Role:** A smuggler who runs salt past the tax at night.

**Profile:**
- Lise and Ivo are lovers, in secret from Mara.
- She needs the light dark for one night each month to bring her boat in.
- Her speech is low and careful, all questions.

### Hallam Reed

**Role:** Old Hallam, the keeper before Mara, who trained her.

**Profile:**
- Blind in his left eye.
- He still climbs to the gallery to listen to the bells.

Whether Hallam saw who cut the *Grey Wren*'s anchor line is never settled.

---

## Relationship Map

- **Mara** and **Ivo**: brother and sister; she has paid his debts twice before.
- **Mara** and **Brack**: he signs her renewal for the Board each spring.
- **Hallam** and **Mara**: he was her teacher, and she visits him every Sunday.

---

## POV Decisions

- **Mara:** every scene. The reader should feel the light as a weight.
